# Session planning pass

Planning notes, 1 January 2025.

## Client constraints

The workshop has 18 participants and a 90-minute slot. The recipe is fresh pasta
with tomato sauce. The agreed learning goal is to practise the dough, shaping
and sauce steps together.

## Onsite arrangement

Use six groups of three in the venue's shared kitchen. Demonstrate each step,
then let the groups practise at their stations. These are planning choices for
the onsite format, not a claim that the same setup works online.
