# Logistics and safety planning pass

Planning notes, 1 January 2025.

## Venue provision

The venue supplies six workstations, the cooking equipment, and ingredients.
Ingredients can be portioned at the venue before participants arrive.

## Instructor plan

The instructor gives a shared demonstration and can see the six workstations.
The safety briefing covers knives, hot water and hob use in that shared space.

## Still to ask

Participant dietary restrictions have not yet been collected. There is no
confirmed list of equipment or ingredients available in participants' homes.
A later move online therefore requires judgment about activities, preparation
and supervision; it does not prove the workshop impossible.
