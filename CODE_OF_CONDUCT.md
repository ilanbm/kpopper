# Code of Conduct

kpopper welcomes people of all backgrounds and levels of experience. This code applies
to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces, and when someone officially
represents the project.

## How we work together

- Be respectful and considerate. Disagree with ideas and explain your reasoning.
- Give constructive feedback and accept corrections. Make room for newcomers and
  questions asked in good faith.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks and publishing
someone else's private information without permission are unacceptable. Retaliation
against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

## Reporting a concern

**Before public release: the maintainer must add a private reporting address here.**

Do not post sensitive incident details in a public issue. A report should describe what
happened, include relevant links or evidence, and say how to contact you. Share only the
personal information needed to investigate.

Reports are handled by the project maintainer, [Ilan Bar Magen](https://github.com/ilanbm).
Information will be shared only as needed to investigate and respond. If a report concerns
the maintainer, or you prefer to contact GitHub directly, use
[GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content and restrict participation. Responses
depend on the severity and pattern of behavior: a request to correct it, a warning,
a temporary restriction or a permanent ban. Serious threats or harassment may result
in an immediate ban. Moderation decisions should explain the reason to the people
involved when doing so is safe and appropriate.
